# Orunmila trademark policy

Version 1.0. Effective 1 December 2026.

Orunmila's code is free and open source under the Apache License 2.0. Its name
is not. This policy explains how you may use the name, so that people can always
tell the real Orunmila from a changed copy.

## Who owns the marks

KALKU DIGITAL SOLUTIONS LIMITED owns the Orunmila trademarks.

## Which marks this covers

- The name **ORUNMILA**, in any capitalisation and with or without accents, used
  for software, a service or anything connected with this project.
- Any Orunmila logo, once one is published.
- The project's names on package registries and code hosts: the npm package
  `orunmila`, the repository `kalkudiary-ai/orunmila`, and the same name on
  other registries (PyPI, Docker Hub and the like), domains and social handles.

The marks are unregistered trademarks (™). We will use ® only for a mark once
it is registered.

## The name's meaning comes first

Ọ̀rúnmìlà is the orisha of wisdom and divination in the Yoruba tradition of Ifá.
This policy is about the name of this software only. It claims nothing over
the name in its religious, cultural or everyday use, and never will.

## The Apache License does not cover the name

Section 6 of the Apache License 2.0 says the licence grants no permission to use
the licensor's trade names or trademarks, except to describe where the work came
from and to reproduce the NOTICE file. You may use, change and share the code
under the licence. The rights to the name come only from this policy.

## What you may do without asking

- Say true things about Orunmila: "built on Orunmila", "works with Orunmila",
  "a fork of Orunmila".
- Share copies of Orunmila that you have not changed, under its own name.
- Link to the project, write about it, review it, teach it or compare it.
- Use the name in community and educational work about the project: talks,
  tutorials, courses, blog posts, meetups.

When you do, please:

- not suggest that we endorse, sponsor or partner with you, unless we do;
- write the name as plain text, not styled like an Orunmila logo;
- say "not affiliated with Orunmila" where a reader could think you are.

## What needs our written permission

- Using the name for a changed version or a fork, including names such as
  "Orunmila Pro", "Orunmila Enterprise" or "Orunmila Plus".
- Using the name for a paid product or a paid version.
- A name that could be confused with Orunmila.
- The name in a domain, an app store listing, a social handle or a package name.
- Merchandise with the name or a logo.
- A hosted service that presents itself as the official Orunmila.

## Forks and changed versions

You are free to fork and change the code. If you share a changed version, give
it a different name, remove any Orunmila logo and branding, and do not call it
Orunmila. You may say it is "based on Orunmila".

## Logos

No Orunmila logo has been published yet. When one is, its files will sit in a
`branding/` folder with their own guidelines (clear space, colours, minimum
size), and they will not be under the Apache License 2.0. Use a logo only as
supplied: never change its shape, colours or proportions, and never combine it
with another mark.

## Asking for permission

For commercial use, partnerships, or any use this policy does not cover, write
to kalkudiary@gmail.com and tell us what you plan to do. Permission is given in
writing, for a stated use.

## Enforcement

We may withdraw a permission given under this policy, and ask for a use that
breaks it to be changed or taken down. We would rather talk first, and usually
will.
